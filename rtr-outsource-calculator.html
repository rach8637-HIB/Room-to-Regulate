Ways to make it cost less

  • Dependent care FSA. If your employer offers one, it can use pre-tax money for after-school care and sitters while you work. Ask HR what your plan covers.
  • Medicaid waivers. Many states fund respite care for kids with disabilities through waiver programs, sometimes regardless of family income. Waitlists are common, so it's worth applying early. See how it works in Shift
  • Trade with another family. Swapping a few hours of care with a family you trust costs nothing but coordination.
