Questions

Before you join

Is the free content going away?

No. Essays, most templates previews, and the policy tracker stay free, always. Membership is for the deeper toolkit, the community, and live access — not a paywall on empathy.

What if I only care about templates, not the community part?

Totally fine — plenty of members mostly lurk and download. No minimum participation required.

Do I have to pick a label to join?

No. Tags are optional and just help you find relevant threads faster. You can join without identifying as anything more specific than "here for the room."

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — monthly cancels anytime, no questions. Annual is refundable within the first 30 days if it's not the right fit.
