The Room

Video guidance for the physical space you regulate in — from people who actually know what they're talking about

Video library

Room to Regulate is a feeling before it's ever a physical space — but for a lot of families, the actual room matters too. A corner that signals "safe to fall apart here." A layout that doesn't overwhelm an already-overwhelmed nervous system. This page collects video guidance from people who've spent real time thinking about that — decluttering for mental health, calming room design, nervous-system-focused movement, and therapists who work with space and environment directly.
