Our standards

These protect our readers, and they're why placements here perform.

  • Every placement is clearly labeled "Presented by" or "Sponsored," and affiliate relationships are disclosed next to the link.
  • Partners don't review or approve editorial content.
  • One partner per category per placement, so your message isn't crowded out.
  • Comparison listings are ranked by fit for families, never by payment.
  • No ad pixels or retargeting on our tools. Reporting is aggregate; we never share individual reader data.
  • Shift, our policy pillar, doesn't accept sponsors.
  • We don't accept products claiming to treat or cure autism or ADHD, diet or weight-loss products, alcohol, gambling, or high-interest lending.
  • We may decline or end any partnership that conflicts with these standards.
