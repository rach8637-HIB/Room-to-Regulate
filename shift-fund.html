The Shift Fund

Make room for the next family.

Support should feel reachable. The Shift Fund is our way of inviting people who can give a little more to help widen that circle.

What is The Shift Fund?

The Shift Fund is a giving initiative connected to Room to Regulate. At checkout, you can choose to add a donation alongside your membership. Giving is optional, and you can join without adding anything.

We are shaping how the fund will support families and this community. We’ll share the specific uses and updates here as those plans are finalized.

How giving works

Choose an amount at checkout, or choose no donation. Any amount you add appears separately from your membership price in the order summary and receipt. You’ll see your total before you pay.

Your place here does not depend on what you give. The essays and free content remain available without a donation.

Stay close as this grows

We’ll use this page to explain what The Shift Fund supports and share updates as the program takes shape. For now, thank you for helping us imagine a community where more families can find their footing.
